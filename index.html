How this works
  1. Type anything you need to do in the bottom box and press the arrow. Times you write are picked up: "before 7", "at 21:00".
  2. The app shows you one task at a time and says why it chose that one.
  3. Done finishes it. Not this one needs a reason, and brings the next task.
  4. Tick Every day while typing for something like washing clothes. It comes back daily, 15 minutes before its time.
  5. In Plan, say what waits for what. If the CV waits for the website, you'll never be handed the CV first.
  6. Also in Plan: mark a step as waiting on someone, with a date. It sleeps until then.
